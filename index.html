Threat Environment

The adversary calculus has changed

China and Russia have restructured their military-industrial complexes around technologies that directly contest U.S. advantages — autonomous systems, electronic warfare, hypersonics, and counter-space capabilities. Transnational criminal organizations have absorbed battlefield technologies from Ukraine and applied them against U.S. interests in the western hemisphere. The pace of adversary adaptation now exceeds the pace of U.S. acquisition.

"Speed of adoption is now a more decisive variable than technical superiority alone."

Global Posture

Projecting U.S. objectives across every AOR

Security cooperation is not aid. It is the mechanism through which the United States shapes the security environment before conflict — building partner capacity, establishing interoperability, and countering adversary influence at the point of contact.

"The forward edge of U.S. national security is a partner nation officer who was trained last year and equipped the year before."
— REI Security Cooperation Framework, 2026
